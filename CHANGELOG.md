## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added
- The sort orders **Price: Low to High** (`price_asc`) and **Price: High to Low** (`price_desc`), each always in its
  own direction, on the field the platform's Price sort uses for the shopper's customer group and website.
- The setting **Enabled** per store view.
